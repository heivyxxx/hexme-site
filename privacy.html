Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. Who We Are

hexme stats is a Telegram bot that provides Fortnite game statistics. Contact us at: hexmeshop3@mail.ru

2. What Data We Collect

When you connect your Epic Games account through our service, we receive the following data via Epic Account Services:

  • Your Epic Account ID
  • Your Epic display name (username)

We do not collect your password, email address, payment information, or any other personal data.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your Epic account data solely to retrieve and display your Fortnite statistics within the Telegram bot. We do not sell, share, rent, or transfer your data to any third parties.

4. Data Storage

Your Epic Account ID and display name are stored securely on our servers and used exclusively to provide the statistics service. We retain this data until you request deletion.

5. How to Delete Your Data

You can delete all your data at any time by sending the /unlink command to the bot. All associated data will be deleted immediately and automatically.

You can also revoke our application's access to your Epic account at any time via your Epic Games account settings at epicgames.com/account/apps.

6. Third-Party Services

We access Epic Account Services in compliance with the Epic Games Developer Agreement. Statistics data is provided by fortnite-api.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 years of age.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the bot after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at hexmeshop3@mail.ru.
